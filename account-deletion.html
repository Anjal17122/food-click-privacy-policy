Delete Your Account

If you would like to delete your account from our Food Ordering App, please follow the instructions below:

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the app
  2. Login
  3. Go to Profile / Settings
  4. Select Delete Account
  5. Confirm your request

If you are unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us at:

anjalsapkota09@gmail.com

Data Deletion

When you delete your account:

  • Your personal information (name, email, phone number) will be deleted
  • Your order history will be removed
  • Your account will be permanently deactivated

Retention Policy

Some data (such as transaction records) may be retained for legal or financial compliance for a limited period.

Processing Time

Your account will be deleted within 7 working days of the request.
